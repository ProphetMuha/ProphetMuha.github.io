Understanding Computer Servers

Computer Servers have made many of the digital services people use every day. They store information, host websites, run applications, and even connect users across networks. This website explores and explains the history, different types, key concepts, and importance of servers

Explore the Website

Important Key Terms

Server

A computer system that provides information, resources, or services for other computers.

Client

A device or program that requests information or service from a server.

Cloud Computing

The delivery of storage, software, and resources for computing over the internet.
